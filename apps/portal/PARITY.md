## Known differences from the drafts (and why)

- **Status page, phone:** the "Application ... Copy sent to" line is 13px (was 16px, the same as body text), at Dominick's ask on 2026-10-11. The Figma phone frame still shows 16px until it is updated. On phone the application number moved out of that line to a small footer line at the bottom of the page (13px, hairline above), also at his ask. The line now reads "Confirmation sent to" (the email is a receipt, not a copy of the answers). Live pages show it only when the email service is set up (RESEND_API_KEY and PORTAL_EMAIL_FROM); demo pages always show it, as the drafts do. The "Your application (PDF)" link is removed on both widths (it only opened the print dialog); his call, 2026-10-11.

- **Short answers (step 3):** the essay step became three required short answers (400 words each, raised from 250 the same day) on 2026-10-12, built in code first. The Figma frames for step 3 (laptop, phone, stress) and the review rows were redrawn to match the same day; before-copies are on the page "Archive: Portal before short answers".
- **No pasting in the short answers (2026-10-12):** paste, drop and yank are blocked in the three boxes so students type their own answers. The lead adds "Pasting is turned off, so type them here."; a blocked paste or drop shows the notice "Pasting is turned off. Type your answer here." under that box (weight 600, no error icon or border; a real error wins). The Figma stress frames show the notice on question 3, and so does `?demo=stress`.
- **Short-answer text boxes:** typed blank lines between paragraphs are taller than the old essay draft's 12px paragraph gap.
- **Inputs:** long values are clipped at the field edge (as the stress frames draw them), not shortened with an ellipsis.
